# Product Specification

## Product

Claude Foundations Lab — an interactive study platform for Claude Certified Associate – Foundations.

## Problem

Passive reading is insufficient for a learner who wants both deep conceptual understanding and certification-style practice. The product should combine explanatory lessons, visual mental models, scenario-based practice, and exam simulation.

## Target learner

A technically curious learner who prefers Vietnamese explanations while retaining English certification terminology and practicing in English.

## Product principles

1. Understand first; memorize second.
2. Visuals must improve comprehension, not merely decorate the page.
3. Practice should test judgment in realistic scenarios.
4. Explain why the correct answer is right and why distractors are wrong.
5. Make learning progress visible without requiring an account.
6. Distinguish official source material from original learning content.

## MVP capabilities

- Learning dashboard and curriculum map.
- Domain and lesson pages.
- Deep Vietnamese lesson content.
- Interactive diagrams and comparisons.
- English lesson quizzes.
- Domain practice and mixed practice.
- Timed mock exam aligned to the verified official format.
- Results and domain breakdown.
- Incorrect-answer review and bookmarks.
- Searchable English–Vietnamese glossary.
- Progress and settings pages.
- Local persistence and reset controls.

## Non-goals

- Backend, account system, cloud sync, payments, or leaderboard.
- Calling Claude API during ordinary study.
- Claiming that generated practice questions are official exam items.
- Guaranteeing a passing score.

## Success criteria

- Every verified exam objective maps to lessons and assessments.
- Core learner flows work on desktop and mobile.
- Question scoring and persistence are covered by tests.
- No dead controls or placeholder content in shipped routes.
